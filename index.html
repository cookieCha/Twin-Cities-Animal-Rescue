Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue exists to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals in need throughout the Twin Cities community. We believe every dog, cat, and small animal deserves a safe home, a loving family, and a chance at a happy life. Through adoption, foster care, and community partnership, we work every day to reduce animal homelessness and build a more compassionate community for animals and people alike.

Our Impact

Since we began our work, our community of adopters, fosters, and volunteers has helped us achieve:

  • 1,200+ animals placed in loving homes
  • 350+ active foster families
  • 98% adoption placement success rate
  • 40+ community and shelter partners across the metro area
  • 15 years serving the Twin Cities region

How You Can Help

There are many ways to support our mission, no matter how much time you have to give:

  • Adopt: Open your home to a rescued animal in need.
  • Foster: Provide temporary care while an animal waits for its forever home.
  • Volunteer: Help at events, transports, and at our facility.
  • Donate: Support food, medical care, and shelter costs.
  • Spread the Word: Share our mission with your friends and family.
