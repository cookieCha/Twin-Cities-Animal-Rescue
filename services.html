Adoption Process

Bringing home a rescued animal is simple and supportive from start to finish:

  1. Browse available animals and submit an adoption interest form.
  2. Meet your potential new pet at one of our adoption events or our facility.
  3. Complete a short adoption application and home compatibility conversation.
  4. Finalize adoption paperwork and pay the adoption fee, which covers vaccinations and spay/neuter surgery.
  5. Bring your new family member home, with post-adoption support available whenever you need it.

Foster Program

Our foster program gives animals a safe, loving environment while they wait for their forever homes. Fosters provide temporary housing, basic care, and companionship, and we provide all supplies, medical care, and ongoing support. Foster commitments can range from a few days to several months depending on the animal's needs. Fostering is a great option for people who want to help animals without a long-term commitment, and it's one of the most impactful ways to increase our capacity to save lives.

Volunteer Roles

We rely on volunteers to keep our organization running. Current roles include:

  • Adoption Event Support: Help greet visitors and showcase adoptable animals at community events.
  • Animal Care Volunteer: Assist with feeding, walking, and enrichment at our facility.
  • Transport Volunteer: Drive animals to vet appointments, foster homes, or adoption events.
  • Administrative Volunteer: Support intake, scheduling, and donor communications.
  • Photography Volunteer: Take photos of adoptable animals to help them find homes faster.
