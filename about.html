Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue was founded by a small group of neighbors who began fostering stray and surrendered animals out of their own homes. What started as an informal network of animal lovers has grown into a trusted community nonprofit serving the greater Twin Cities area. Over the years we have partnered with local shelters, veterinary clinics, and municipalities to reduce euthanasia rates and connect animals with families who will love them for life. Today, our work is powered by a dedicated staff, hundreds of volunteers, and a community that believes every animal deserves a chance.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion: We treat every animal and every person with kindness and respect.
  • Transparency: We are honest and open about our work, our impact, and our needs.
  • Community: We believe lasting change happens when neighbors work together.
  • Accountability: We are responsible stewards of donor trust and animal welfare.
  • Hope: We believe every animal, no matter their story, deserves a second chance.
